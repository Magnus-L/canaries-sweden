LIMITS, which belong in the paper and not only here:
  Both sources are stratified sample surveys with weights, and ITFtg_Stora covers only firms with ten or more employees. This validates the measure on the larger-firm part of our panel, not on all of it.
  A first stage says the score predicts reported adoption. It does not make adoption exogenous, and it does not turn the age contrast into a causal estimate of AI.
