The exposure percentile is the DAIOE generative-AI index the employment design uses. Teleworkable is the \citet{dingel2020many} classification on the route of Section~\ref{sec:posting_rivals}: the share of the US occupations mapped to a Swedish occupation that can be done entirely at home. Each level is the employment-weighted mean over the group's occupational distribution, and each gap is women minus men. Gap (SD) divides the gap by the standard deviation of the score across occupations, weighted by the employment of all employees aged 16 to 64 (28.8 percentile points and 42.6 percentage points), so the two gaps are in the same units. Corr. is the correlation of the two scores across occupations, weighted by the age band's employment. The paper's young band, 22 to 25, straddles the register's first two bands. Like Table~\ref{tab:occ_mix_by_sex}, this is the national distribution and states a difference in the occupations the two sexes hold; it does not decompose the estimated differential.
