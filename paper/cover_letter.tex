\begin{letter}{The Editor\\Economics Letters}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

Enclosed for your consideration is an original research paper entitled ``Same Storm, Different Boats: Generative AI and the Age Gradient in Hiring''.

The paper investigates whether the post-2022 divergence between stock prices and job postings reflects generative AI or monetary tightening, and what each channel implies for who is being hired. The two episodes coincided in 2022, which makes the channels difficult to separate from aggregate data alone. The Riksbank's first rate hike came in April 2022, seven months before ChatGPT launched in November 2022, and we use this seven-month gap as a within-Sweden timing test for whether the differential posting decline began with monetary tightening or with generative AI. We combine the full population of Swedish job advertisements (2020 through 2026) with monthly employer-declaration register data covering all employed individuals (2019 through 2025), using an employer-level difference-in-differences design with employer$\times$month fixed effects.

The aggregate posting decline tracks monetary tightening, not AI. Within employers, however, employment of workers aged 22 to 25 in AI-exposed occupations falls 5.5 per cent by mid-2025, concentrated among young women, mainly through reduced hiring, and accelerating only after ChatGPT. Population-level employment remains stable, so the pattern is one of reallocation rather than net destruction. The contribution is this reconciliation: aggregate vacancy declines reflect monetary tightening, while AI reshapes hiring composition inside firms.

The data combine monthly frequency, full-population employer-employee linkage from Statistics Sweden, and SSYK 4-digit occupational codes. The posting analysis is based on publicly available advertisements (CC0 licence) and the replication code is openly archived.

The body is 1{,}929 words (within the 2{,}000 word limit) with an 89 word abstract and an Online Appendix. The work is original and unpublished, and all authors approve the submission.

Thank you for your consideration.

\closing{Yours sincerely,}
